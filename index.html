About Me

Excellence in Every Frame

Elizabeth Chinaza Ojiabor, known as Mc Ecool, is a dynamic media personality and corporate leader based in Lagos, Nigeria. With unmatched charisma and communication prowess, she dominates as an MC, TV presenter, red-carpet host, actress, and filmmaker.

As COO of Kowi X Tech and National VP 1 at Cyon Nigeria, she merges creativity with strategy. Her platform FromTheRootNaija champions authentic African storytelling.
